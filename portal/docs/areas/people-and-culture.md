## Why a different staffing model?

Tarbiyat combines recognised academic learning, Mission Labs, mentoring and authentic external work. A conventional **teacher-only** workforce is unlikely to carry all of that well. Domain experts alone are also not enough — knowing a profession is not the same as teaching children safely and well.

## Three faculty functions (not ranks)

| Function | In plain terms |
|---|---|
| **Educator** | Designs and leads learning: teaching, sequencing, misconceptions, formative assessment, accessibility and Formal Recognition requirements |
| **Practitioner** | Brings current/recent professional expertise into labs and missions — across **all Six Worlds**, not only engineering |
| **Mentor** | Holds a serious longitudinal relationship: reflection, pathway dialogue, wellbeing observation and referral — **not** a therapist by default |

These are **functions**. One person may hold more than one when prepared and authorised. Nobody is “Tier-1” above anyone else.

## Who else is needed?

- **Technical / Lab Professionals** — technicians, workshop and safety specialists, and similar roles. They are an essential **supporting workforce**, not a fourth faculty function, and they hold real safety authority.
- **Specialist Professional Services** — counselling, safeguarding leads, nursing, SEN, librarians, residential house staff (if residential), career/IP/data specialists. These are **not** folded into “Mentor.”

## How adults work together

Tarbiyat is designed around **professional learning teams**, not one heroic classroom teacher.

A substantial Mission may draw, **proportionally**:

Educator + Practitioner + Mentor + Technical Professional + External Expert (when needed)

Younger learners usually need more continuity with Educators and Mentors. Advanced Deepen/Produce work may involve denser specialist Practitioner support.

## Hard boundaries (examples)

- A brilliant engineer does **not** automatically replace a teacher.
- A Mentor does **not** unilaterally decide a student’s pathway.
- Partner commercial deadlines do **not** override Education-First.
- Mentors refer serious mental-health concerns; they do not impersonate clinicians.
- External Institution-in-Residence people are **not** automatic faculty.
