## Architectural chain

**Mission → Educationalisation → Supervised production / Campus Live Workflow → Evidence → optional Deployment**

**External partner → [MIS-001](/areas/mission-partner-governance) (Education-First) → optional [MIS-002](/areas/institution-in-residence) (sustained embedding) → Mission risk review (GAP-024)**

Guest ≠ Mission Partner ≠ Institution / Industry-in-Residence. “In residence” means sustained educational embedding, not sponsorship or physical tenancy alone.
