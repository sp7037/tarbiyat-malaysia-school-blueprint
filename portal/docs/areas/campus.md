## What Core now answers

- Campus includes **governed external** capability — not only owned rooms
- **Six Worlds ≠ six laboratories**
- Launch essentials are **functions**, not flagship capital
- Facilities must be **capacity-coupled** and **anti-theatre**
- Learning-time modes need convertible Knowledge Studio / Mission environments

## Minimum Viable Tarbiyat Campus (functional)

Flexible Knowledge/Mission environments + safe specialist access (on-site and/or external) + mentoring/critique + basic support & safeguarding controls + formation access + governed external network.
