<section class="present-section">
  <h2>Why this model exists</h2>
  <p>
    Conventional schooling often separates academic subjects from the work of investigating, making and
    contributing. Tarbiyat is intended as a <strong>distinct organising architecture for schooling</strong> —
    not merely better equipment added to a period-based timetable.
  </p>
  <p>
    The lived experience is progressively organised around laboratories, studios, workshops, missions and
    demonstrated capability. Books, theory, languages, mathematics practice and examination preparation remain
    where they are pedagogically appropriate. Tarbiyat is Lab-First, not lab-only.
  </p>
</section>

<section class="present-section">
  <h2>Core learning architecture</h2>
  <div class="present-grid present-grid--3">
    <article class="present-card">
      <h3>Lab-First</h3>
      <p>Investigation and production environments organise substantial learning. Knowledge Studios support theory, critique and practice — they are not the exclusive centre of school life.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="present-card">
      <h3>Mission-Based</h3>
      <p>Sustained missions with real or realistically scoped problems organise learning effort toward inspectable outcomes, not only confirmation practicals.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="present-card">
      <h3>Dual recognition</h3>
      <p>Recognised academic attainment and Tarbiyat capability evidence are complementary. Capability records do not replace legally required credentials.</p>
    </article>
  </div>

</section>

<section class="present-section">
  <h2>How students progress</h2>
  <p>
    Development follows a working progression of stages — exploration before strong specialisation:
  </p>
  <div class="stage-rail" aria-label="Developmental stages">
    <span>Explore</span>
    <span class="stage-rail__arrow" aria-hidden="true">→</span>
    <span>Discover</span>
    <span class="stage-rail__arrow" aria-hidden="true">→</span>
    <span>Choose</span>
    <span class="stage-rail__arrow" aria-hidden="true">→</span>
    <span>Deepen</span>
    <span class="stage-rail__arrow" aria-hidden="true">→</span>
    <span>Produce</span>
  </div>
  <p>
    These are developmental stages for the whole student journey. They are not the same thing as
    how deep a learner has gone in a particular domain.
  </p>
  <p class="present-more"><a href="/student-journey">Student Journey →</a></p>
</section>

<section class="present-section">
  <h2>Six Worlds</h2>
  <p>
    Learning is organised across six developmental professional domains of equal dignity —
    not career labels for children, not school houses, and not substitutes for academic subjects.
  </p>
  <ol class="world-list">
    <li>Engineering &amp; Intelligent Systems</li>
    <li>Health &amp; Life Sciences</li>
    <li>Enterprise &amp; Economics</li>
    <li>Earth, Energy &amp; Built Environment</li>
    <li>Creative, Media &amp; Human Communication</li>
    <li>Society, Leadership &amp; Public Systems</li>
  </ol>
  <p class="present-status-note">World capability architecture is drafted and under review — not yet approved.</p>
  <p class="present-more"><a href="/six-worlds">Six Worlds →</a></p>
</section>

<section class="present-section">
  <h2>Missions that produce authentic work</h2>
  <p>
    Missions connect learning to real or realistically scoped problems. They can cross Worlds, involve
    partners under education-first governance, and generate evidence of what students can actually do.
  </p>
  <p class="present-status-note">Mission Ecosystem detail is under review; Lab-First / Mission-Based principles are approved.</p>
  <p class="present-more"><a href="/missions">Missions →</a></p>
</section>

<section class="present-section">
  <h2>Proof of Capability</h2>
  <p>
    Alongside recognised academic credentials, Tarbiyat records evidence-backed Capability Claims —
    with context, contribution honesty, verification and human governance.
  </p>
  <p>
    Proof of Capability is not a universal score, not a badge farm, and not a replacement for
    legally recognised academic qualifications.
  </p>
  <p class="present-status-note">Dual assessment and Proof of Capability Core architecture are approved; POC-001 operating pack remains DRAFT.</p>
  <p class="present-more"><a href="/proof-of-capability">Proof of Capability →</a></p>
</section>
