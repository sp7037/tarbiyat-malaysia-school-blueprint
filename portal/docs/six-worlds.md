# Six Worlds

<p class="present-lede">
  Six Worlds organise exploration, missions and later concentration.
  They are domains of equal dignity — not career labels, school houses or subject lists.
</p>

<div class="planned-banner">
  <strong>UNDER REVIEW — architecture drafted; not approved</strong><br/>
  Worlds are named in the Concept Constitution. Capability frames and progressive depth are proposed in ADR-0011.
  GAP-008 is proposed for resolution via ADR-0011 — not closed.
</div>

## What a World is

A World is a developmental learning and professional **domain**: a lens for exploration and concentration, a source of Missions, an organising structure for later Major + Minor, and a context for capability development.

Worlds organise **capability and mission context**. Academic subjects may support Worlds; Worlds are not conventional subject lists in disguise.

## What Worlds are not

- Permanent career labels for children
- School houses or identity tribes
- Six compulsory vocational streams
- Legal professions students are licensed to practise
- Rigid substitutes for recognised academic disciplines
- A prestige hierarchy (no World is architecturally “above” another)

## Capability depth ladder

Shared across Worlds, independent of whole-student developmental stage:

<div class="depth-rail" aria-label="Capability depth ladder">
  <span>Encounter</span>
  <span class="depth-rail__arrow" aria-hidden="true">→</span>
  <span>Inquire</span>
  <span class="depth-rail__arrow" aria-hidden="true">→</span>
  <span>Apply</span>
  <span class="depth-rail__arrow" aria-hidden="true">→</span>
  <span>Integrate</span>
  <span class="depth-rail__arrow" aria-hidden="true">→</span>
  <span>Advance</span>
  <span class="depth-rail__arrow" aria-hidden="true">→</span>
  <span>Contribute</span>
</div>

| Depth | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Encounter | Recognise the domain |
| Inquire | Guided inquiry and making |
| Apply | Bounded problem application |
| Integrate | Combine capabilities; rising complexity |
| Advance | Substantial independence; authentic standards |
| Contribute | Defensible advanced output |

**Stage ≠ capability depth.** This ladder is not a universal scoring system, grade conversion table or badge sequence.

## Cross-World Missions

Real problems often span Worlds. Missions may intentionally integrate more than one World. Not every Mission must touch all six.

## Progressive relationship to stages

| Stage | World relationship (proposed) |
|---|---|
| Explore | Worlds mainly as adult curriculum-design tools |
| Discover | Meaningful exposure across all Six Worlds |
| Choose | Provisional concentration without lock-in |
| Deepen | Major + Minor within or across Worlds |
| Produce | Advanced authentic contribution, including Cross-World work |
