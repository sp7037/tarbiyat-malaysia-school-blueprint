Data Sovereignty — Corporate Partner Isolation
Encryption
AES-256 at rest · TLS 1.3 in transit
All data on campus — student records, mission IP, corporate R&D datasets, operational systems — encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. No plaintext data anywhere in the sovereign network.
Network Isolation
4 Physically Separate Segments
Sovereign Academic Network · Corporate Partner VLAN (isolated per partner) · Campus Operations Network · Administrative Network. No routing between segments. Inter-segment traffic passes through dual NGFW with deep packet inspection.
Geo-Sovereignty
Zero Data Outside Oman
Geo-blocking enforced at NGFW level: all non-Omani IP ranges blocked by default except explicitly whitelisted government and academic partners. All data on on-campus Tier-2 Data Centre owned 100% by Government of Oman. No cloud storage — ever.
