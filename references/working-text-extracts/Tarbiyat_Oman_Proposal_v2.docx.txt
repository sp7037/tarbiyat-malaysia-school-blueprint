THE BIG IDEA Tarbiyat National Youth Tech Missions School & College is Oman's first Government-Owned, World-Class National Youth R&D Campus. From Grade 5 through a post-Grade 12 applied technology diploma, every Omani student on this campus becomes a junior researcher, innovator, and nation-builder — solving real problems for the Sultanate. The campus is built to world-class infrastructure standards: from AI-powered labs, a grand auditorium, full boarding (hostel) facilities, 24×7 solar-backed power, and a Nizwa Fort-inspired architectural identity. It is not comparable to any existing school in Oman or the wider region.
