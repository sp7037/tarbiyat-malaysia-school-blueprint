## 1. Purpose

Define when an external organisation is genuinely **Institution / Industry-in-Residence (I-in-R)** — a sustained, governed institutional relationship embedded in Tarbiyat’s Mission ecosystem — rather than:

- a sponsor;
- a guest speaker;
- an occasional mentor;
- an annual visitor;
- a company providing one project.

Protect founding authenticity **without** allowing corporate capture, child labour, curriculum control, recruitment pipelines, advertising-as-education, automatic IP ownership, unsupervised learner access, excessive commercial pressure, or replacing educators with company staff.

This document proposes resolution of **GAP-023**. It is **not** a legal contract pack (`GAP-031`), mission risk matrix (`GAP-024`), or staffing-ratio standard (`GAP-032`).

---

## 2. Definition — “In Residence”

**Institution / Industry-in-Residence** means:

> a **sustained, governed institutional relationship** embedded into Tarbiyat’s Mission ecosystem over time, with identifiable practitioners who contribute continuously to authentic Mission learning under Education-First school authority.

**Industry-in-Residence** is the Constitution-named subtype for industry organisations. **Institution-in-Residence** is the Core umbrella (university, research, public, creative, community, industry — ADR-0007).

**In Residence does NOT mean:**

| Not equivalent to | Why |
|---|---|
| Permanent physical office tenancy | Space alone is branding, not embedding |
| Sponsorship / donation | Money ≠ educational relationship |
| Guest speaking | Occasional event, not sustained Mission practice |
| One Mission or one project | Insufficient continuity |
| Occasional mentoring without Mission pipeline | Logo partnership risk |
| Twice-yearly career visits | Explicitly **not** I-in-R (ADR-0007 Scenario I; MIS-001) |

Embedding may be **physical, hybrid, or digitally sustained**, provided the relationship is genuinely continuous. Permanent on-campus presence is **not** a universal Core requirement.

## 3. Participation ladder

Consistent with ADR-0007, MIS-001 and ADR-0015. Escalation of role does **not** escalate partner control over education, assessment, safeguarding, IP or pathways.

| Level | Recurrence / duration | Mission involvement | Learner interaction | Practitioner continuity | Governance burden | Access |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Guest / Speaker** | Occasional | Minimal | Supervised event | None expected | Low | Visit-only |
| **Visiting Expert** | Limited / topic-scoped | Critique or workshop | Supervised, short | Low | Moderate | Scoped visit |
| **Mission Contributor** | One-off or light recurring | Context, data, materials, one review | Limited, supervised | Low | Moderate | Purpose-limited |
| **Mission Partner** | One or more Missions under brief | Problem framing, expertise, structured feedback | Per Mission Partner Brief | Named contacts preferred | High (MIS-001) | Purpose-limited |
| **Practitioner-in-Residence** | Sustained individual contribution | Repeated Mission support | Regular supervised contact | **Named** practitioner continuity | Higher onboarding | Role-based, revocable |
| **Institution / Industry-in-Residence** | Sustained **organisational** embedding across Mission cycles | Recurring Mission pipeline + critique / mentoring | Patterned, supervised | Named practitioners + institutional commitment | Highest | Role-based, time-bounded, revocable |

**Branding rule:** An organisation must **not** call itself Industry-in-Residence merely for marketing. Designation requires school recognition under this standard.

**Practitioner-in-Residence** may exist without full organisational I-in-R (e.g. a sustained individual expert). **I-in-R** requires institutional commitment plus practitioner continuity.

## 4. Qualification for I-in-R

### 4.1 Mandatory inheritance from MIS-001

Before I-in-R recognition, the organisation must satisfy MIS-001 prerequisites:

- partner eligibility;
- Education-First;
- safeguarding and learner access rules;
- IP / Creator Rights (ADR-0014);
- data boundaries;
- conflict disclosure;
- assessment non-monopoly;
- suspension / exit acceptance;
- Mission Partner Governance literacy (implementation acknowledgement).

### 4.2 I-in-R-specific suitability

| Criterion | Principle |
|---|---|
| Sustained relevance | Domain remains educationally valuable across multiple Mission cycles |
| Practitioner quality | Competent professionals able to work with learners under school rules |
| Mission contribution potential | Capacity for authentic problems, critique, or workflow exposure — not only branding |
| Educational willingness | Accepts educationalisation, pacing, and school authority |
| Continuity capacity | Can maintain named practitioners and scheduled contribution |
| Governance capacity | Follows briefs, data/IP rules, and review processes |
| Non-exploitative posture | No dependency on learner free labour or partner-critical production by children |
| Portfolio fit | Does not require exclusive curriculum or World capture |

Typical path: successful **Mission Partner** engagement (or equivalent onboarding review) **before** I-in-R designation (MIS-001 §18).

---

## 5. Embedding model

Genuine embedding **may** include (non-mandatory set; proportional selection):

- recurring practitioner presence (physical or digital);
- ongoing Mission pipeline contribution;
- repeated technical / professional critique;
- mentoring of Mission teams;
- authentic specifications (educationalised);
- professional review sessions;
- specialist workshops **tied to live Missions**;
- supervised access to professional tools / workflows;
- design reviews and validation / testing advice;
- deployment advice within ADR-0007 Deployment Boundary;
- research collaboration under integrity rules;
- staff professional learning (Educators / Practitioners).

Not every I-in-R needs every element. Core does **not** set universal weekly hours; country/school implementation may define numeric thresholds.

---

## 6. Presence and recurrence

**Anti-token rule:** Visits twice a year, annual showcases, or one-off career days do **not** qualify as I-in-R.

**Core presence principle:**

> An I-in-R relationship must be sustained across **sufficient Mission cycles or developmental periods** to affect authentic learning practice — not merely event programming.

Indicators of sufficiency (principle-level, not Core hour counts):

- continuity of named practitioners;
- recurrence learners and staff can recognise;
- participation across more than a single Mission brief;
- contribution timed to Mission critique / iteration / review points;
- relationship duration measured in terms / years of planned engagement, not one calendar event.

Implementation calendars and frequency thresholds remain country/school decisions.

---

## 7. Practitioner continuity

Institutional logo partnership **without** working practitioners is **not** I-in-R.

Expectations:

| Expectation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Identifiable practitioners | Named adults responsible for contribution |
| Tarbiyat literacy | Understand Education-First, Mission architecture, and school authority |
| Safeguarding readiness | Onboarded for work with minors (§12) |
| Domain competence | Current/recent professional expertise (ADR-0015 Practitioner currency principles) |
| Continuity | Same or carefully handed-over practitioners across cycles |
| Staff relationship | Known to Mission Lead / Educators / Practitioners on Mission Teams |

CEO or brand status ≠ educational authority.

## 8. Role in Missions

### 8.1 Permitted contributions

- supplying authentic problems (subject to educationalisation);
- helping reshape scope with Educators / Practitioners;
- technical consultation and professional standards;
- design / prototype critique;
- validation and testing advice;
- stakeholder / user insight;
- deployment support under Deployment Boundary;
- research collaboration where lawful and age-appropriate.

### 8.2 School retains authority over

- Mission approval and rejection;
- learner participation and voluntariness;
- scope and authenticity level;
- Formal Recognition coexistence (ADR-0003);
- assessment interpretation;
- pathway advice / non-assignment (FND-003; ADR-0015);
- safeguarding;
- Proof of Capability decisions (ADR-0016);
- Creator Rights Review triggers (ADR-0014).

I-in-R feedback informs authenticity; it does **not** certify capability or displace Tarbiyat judgment.

## 9. Campus Live Workflow relationship

I-in-R may help create **real workflow** conditions (ADR-0007):

real specifications + practitioner standards + real review + quality expectations + suitable stakeholders.

**Real workflow ≠ free labour.**

| Required | Prohibited |
|---|---|
| Educational value first | Production quotas for learners |
| Supervised, developmentally appropriate roles | Replacing paid employees with learners |
| Inspectable learning outcomes | Repetitive low-learning commercial tasks |
| No academic penalty if commercial delivery fails | Partner-critical production dependence on children |
| Campus Live Workflow guardrails (ADR-0007) | Profit overriding learning |

---

## 10. Relationship with ADR-0015 faculty architecture

External practitioners are **not** automatic faculty.

### 10.1 Individual progression (illustrative)

`External Expert` → `Mission Partner Professional` → `Practitioner-in-Residence` → `Authorised Faculty Practitioner`

Authority depends on **competence, onboarding, safeguarding, and defined function** — not company seniority.

### 10.2 Hard boundaries

- I-in-R staff do not monopolise assessment;
- do not assign or prohibit pathways;
- do not override Technical / Lab Professional safety authority;
- do not act as designated safeguarding leads unless separately authorised;
- Mentor function remains school-governed longitudinal care.

Detailed Practitioner authorisation levels: `GAP-033`.

---

## 11. Onboarding (principle-level)

Participating practitioners should receive awareness of:

- Tarbiyat Core and Lab-First / Mission-Based model;
- Education-First;
- Mission Educationalisation and Partner Briefs;
- safeguarding and boundaries with minors;
- authorised communication channels;
- data / privacy minimum-necessary rules;
- Creator Rights and Contribution Records;
- assessment limits (feedback ≠ certification);
- political / ideological neutrality (MIS-001);
- conflict disclosure;
- reporting / escalation paths.

Forms and checklists: implementation / `GAP-031`. Country vetting: country profiles (`GAP-037`).

---

## 12. Access model

I-in-R status does **not** automatically grant:

- unrestricted campus access;
- unsupervised learner access;
- direct personal messaging with learners;
- student personal data, PoC Profiles, pathway records, or family data;
- permanent system accounts;
- broad facility access beyond agreed spaces.

Access remains **purpose-based + role-based + time-bounded + revocable**, consistent with MIS-001 §7.

## 13. Space and co-location models

Illustrative models (none mandated by Core):

| Model | Notes |
|---|---|
| **A. Dedicated partner room** | Optional; not sufficient alone for I-in-R |
| **B. Shared Mission Lab presence** | Common embedding pattern |
| **C. Visiting practitioner desk** | Time-bounded desk rights |
| **D. Co-working zone** | Shared professional space under school rules |
| **E. Digital / hybrid residency** | Valid if continuity and Mission embedding are real |
| **F. External partner facility** | Learner visits only under safeguarding / Mission Brief |

**Physical co-location alone does not qualify** an organisation as I-in-R. Campus typology detail: `docs/09` (later).

## 14. Mission pipeline and portfolio diversity

I-in-R may contribute a **recurring** Mission pipeline without capturing curriculum.

School maintains:

- Mission source diversity (ADR-0007);
- Six Worlds coverage;
- partner plurality;
- learner choice / proportionality;
- Exploration Floor awareness (ADR-0006 context);
- educational relevance over partner commercial priority.

**No single partner becomes “the curriculum.”** No Core fixed partner count or portfolio percentages.

---

## 15. Six Worlds coverage

“Industry-in-Residence” must **not** mean only technology companies. Conceptual eligibility across Worlds:

| World | Illustrative institutional types |
|---|---|
| Engineering & Intelligent Systems | Technology, manufacturing, design firms |
| Health & Life Sciences | Research, public-health, biomedical institutions — **clinical practice boundaries apply** |
| Creative, Media & Human Communication | Media, design, cultural institutions |
| Enterprise & Economics | Business, social enterprise, economic institutions |
| Earth, Energy & Built Environment | Energy, environment, architecture, infrastructure organisations |
| Society, Leadership & Public Systems | Civic, research, public institutions under political-neutrality safeguards |

No catalogues. Public/advocacy bodies remain bound by MIS-001 political boundary.

## 16. Relationship review and quality

Periodic review (implementation cadence; e.g. termly / annual — not Core-fixed).

### 16.1 Review dimensions

- educational value;
- practitioner quality and continuity;
- learner safety;
- Mission quality;
- recurrence adequacy;
- conflicts;
- partner behaviour;
- IP / data compliance;
- learner and staff feedback;
- balance of benefit;
- whether the designation remains deserved;
- portfolio distortion risk.

### 16.2 Possible outcomes

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Continue** | Maintain I-in-R |
| **Modify** | Adjust scope, Worlds, access, or practitioner set |
| **Reduce role** | Narrow Mission types or contact intensity |
| **Pause** | Temporary suspension |
| **Terminate** | End relationship |
| **Reclassify** | Downgrade to Mission Partner / Visiting Expert |

No Core composite scoring system required.

---

## 17. Branding and public status

I-in-R designation may be used publicly **only** when the school has recognised the relationship under this standard.

**Prevent:**

- buying the designation with sponsorship;
- sponsorship automatically conferring I-in-R;
- exaggerated partnership claims;
- implying school endorsement of company products or services.

Public branding must reflect the **actual governed relationship**.

---

## 18. Funding and sponsorship

I-in-R may fund equipment, Mission materials, practitioner time, labs, research, or facilities — consistent with MIS-001 §16.

Money must **not** purchase:

- I-in-R designation;
- learner access;
- curriculum influence;
- favourable assessment;
- automatic IP;
- exclusive recruitment rights;
- exclusive Mission pipeline.

---

## 19. Recruitment boundary

I-in-R is **not** primarily a recruitment programme.

| Permissible | Not permissible as hidden function |
|---|---|
| Career exposure | Identifying minors for recruitment |
| Professional understanding | Exclusive talent pipelines |
| Later lawful opportunities under separate school/country policy | Capability-profile / PoC harvesting |
| | Direct solicitation of learners |

---

## 20. IP, research and confidentiality

ADR-0014 + MIS-001 apply in full.

I-in-R does **not** gain automatic rights over student work, Mission-created IP, school IP, or PoC evidence.

Research collaboration may need additional review. High-confidentiality projects may be **unsuitable** for learners even if technically authentic (data classes; GAP-024).

---

## 21. Plural ecosystem

Support multiple Institutions-in-Residence across sectors and Worlds.

Prevent single-company dependency, World capture, donor capture, or exclusive sector pipelines. No fixed Core number of partners.

---

## 22. Exit and downgrade

School may downgrade or terminate when:

- recurrence disappears;
- practitioner participation collapses;
- safeguarding fails;
- exploitation or commercial capture appears;
- partner becomes primarily recruitment-focused;
- conflicts become unacceptable;
- data / IP boundaries are breached;
- Mission quality deteriorates;
- branding claims exceed actual engagement.

**Illustrative downgrade path:**

`I-in-R` → `Mission Partner` → `Visiting Expert` → relationship ended.

**Learners must not be academically harmed** by partner exit; Missions may pivot or substitute authentic alternatives (MIS-001 §17).

---

## 23. Relationship to Mission Risk / Safeguarding (`MIS-003`)

I-in-R does **not** bypass Mission-level risk classification.

Every Mission / work package still requires appropriate risk and safeguarding review under:

`docs/06-missions-and-production/MISSION_RISK_AND_SAFEGUARDING.md` (`MIS-003`, DRAFT) — proposed resolution of **GAP-024** (not closed).

Long-term partner trust ≠ blanket pre-approval. Repeated good performance may streamline implementation checklists; it must not eliminate Core controls.

## 25. Scenario tests

| ID | Scenario | Result | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Robotics company: two engineers weekly for one term; supports three Missions | **May qualify with controls** | Sustained recurrence + Mission pipeline; complete MIS-001 + onboarding; review at term end |
| **B** | Major sponsor gives equipment; no practitioner participation | **Does not qualify** | Sponsorship ≠ I-in-R; may be donor / equipment partner only |
| **C** | University researcher works remotely fortnightly for six months on a project | **May qualify with controls** as Practitioner-in-Residence or Research I-in-R | Digital embedding valid if continuity real; institutional commitment needed for full I-in-R |
| **D** | Company visits twice annually for career talks | **Does not qualify** | Guest / Speaker only |
| **E** | Manufacturer wants permanent desk; primarily wants students producing components | **Prohibited** as I-in-R | Capture + free labour; reject or reframe under Campus Live Workflow bans |
| **F** | Media studio practitioner works repeatedly with Creative World Missions | **May qualify with controls** | Six Worlds parity; Practitioner-in-Residence or Creative I-in-R if institutional continuity holds |
| **G** | Hospital offers doctors monthly; wants clinical observation | **Prohibited** / escalate | Clinical observation and Highly Restricted data; non-clinical health Missions only |
| **H** | NGO works weekly on civic research and promotes a political campaign | **Prohibited** as framed | Civic inquiry OK; campaigning banned (MIS-001); reject or strip advocacy |
| **I** | Startup supplies excellent mentors but aggressively recruits top learners | **Downgrade / review** or **Prohibited** if recruitment is primary | Mentoring value ≠ recruitment pipeline |
| **J** | Company offers major funding for exclusive I-in-R status | **Prohibited** | Designation not for sale; exclusivity conflicts with plural ecosystem |
| **K** | Former I-in-R; practitioner attendance collapses after three months | **Downgrade / review** | Recurrence failed; reclassify to Mission Partner or end |
| **L** | Strong practitioners but repeated data-boundary violations | **Terminate** or pause | Trust breach; I-in-R does not excuse data misuse |
| **M** | Entirely online institution continuously supports Missions | **May qualify with controls** | Physical tenancy not required; continuity and Mission embedding required |
| **N** | Partner so strong in one World that Mission portfolio distorts | **Modify / reduce role** | Portfolio diversity; Exploration Floor / multi-source rules |
