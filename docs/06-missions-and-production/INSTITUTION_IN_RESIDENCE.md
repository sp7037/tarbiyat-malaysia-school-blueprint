## 1. Purpose

Define when an external organisation is genuinely **Institution / Industry-in-Residence (I-in-R)** — a sustained, governed institutional relationship embedded in Tarbiyat’s Mission ecosystem — rather than a sponsor, guest, vendor, tenant, recruiter, occasional mentor, or one-project company.

Operators should be able to determine:

- what I-in-R is;
- what additional educational value justifies it beyond Mission Partner status;
- what deeper governance applies because of embedding;
- when designation must be refused, restricted, suspended, downgraded or terminated.

Protect founding authenticity **without** allowing corporate capture, child labour, curriculum control, recruitment pipelines, advertising-as-education, automatic IP ownership, unsupervised learner access, excessive commercial pressure, or replacing educators with company staff.

This document resolves **GAP-023** as Core Institution-in-Residence operating architecture. It is **not** a legal contract pack (`MIS-TPL-001` / FRM APPROVED for educational records — not country contracts), Mission risk matrix (`MIS-003` — already APPROVED), or staffing-ratio standard (`GAP-032`).

**Public rule of thumb:** I-in-R means deeper, sustained educational integration — not a mechanism for an outside organisation to control curriculum, learners, data, PoC, IP, campus activity or school governance.

---

## 2. Definition — “In Residence”

**Institution / Industry-in-Residence** means:

> a **sustained, governed institutional relationship** embedded into Tarbiyat’s Mission ecosystem over time, with identifiable practitioners who contribute continuously to authentic Mission learning under Education-First school authority.

**Industry-in-Residence** is the Constitution-named subtype for industry organisations. **Institution-in-Residence** is the Core umbrella (university, research, public, creative, community, industry — ADR-0007).

### 2.1 What I-in-R is not

| Not equivalent to | Why |
|---|---|
| Ordinary **Mission Partner** | Partner may support one or more Missions under brief; I-in-R requires sustained organisational embedding across Mission cycles (§3–§6) |
| **Sponsor / donor** | Money ≠ educational relationship; funding alone never creates I-in-R (§18) |
| **Vendor / supplier** | Procurement of goods/services ≠ Mission embedding |
| **Visiting organisation** | Occasional campus visit under visitor controls |
| **Practitioner** | Individual educational function (ADR-0015 / PEO); institution ≠ Practitioner |
| **Practitioner-in-Residence** | Sustained **individual** contribution; may exist without organisational I-in-R |
| **Employer / recruiter** | Later lawful pathways may exist under separate rules; I-in-R is not a recruitment programme (§19) |
| **Outsourced teaching provider** | Tarbiyat retains educational authority; I-in-R does not replace Educators |
| **Tenant / licensee** merely using campus facilities | Space alone is branding or commercial occupancy — not embedding (§13) |
| Permanent physical office tenancy | Space alone ≠ I-in-R |
| Guest speaking / annual visitor / one Mission | Insufficient continuity (ADR-0007 Scenario I) |
| Famous brand / publicity association | Prestige ≠ educational value (§17, §29) |

Embedding may be **physical, hybrid, or digitally sustained**, provided the relationship is genuinely continuous. Permanent on-campus presence is **not** a universal Core requirement.

### 2.2 Additional value justifying I-in-R

I-in-R status is justified only by **structured educational integration** beyond ordinary Mission Partner engagement, such as:

- sustained authentic disciplinary exposure across Mission cycles;
- recurring Mission pipeline contribution;
- practitioner continuity with named adults;
- tools / facilities / expertise learners can rely on over time;
- repeated critique / mentoring under school governance;
- real-world contexts and workflows that remain Education-First.

**No** organisation obtains I-in-R merely because it: gives funding; has a famous brand; provides equipment; rents space; promises internships; or brings publicity — unless genuine structured educational integration is demonstrated and governed.

I-in-R remains **subordinate** to the school’s educational purpose and governance at all times.

## 3. Participation ladder

Consistent with ADR-0007, APPROVED MIS-001 and ADR-0015. Escalation of role does **not** escalate partner control over education, assessment, safeguarding, IP or pathways.

| Level | Recurrence / duration | Mission involvement | Learner interaction | Practitioner continuity | Governance burden | Access |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Guest / Speaker** | Occasional | Minimal | Supervised event | None expected | Low | Visit-only |
| **Visiting Expert** | Limited / topic-scoped | Critique or workshop | Supervised, short | Low | Moderate | Scoped visit |
| **Mission Contributor** | One-off or light recurring | Context, data, materials, one review | Limited, supervised | Low | Moderate | Purpose-limited |
| **Mission Partner** | One or more Missions under brief | Problem framing, expertise, structured feedback | Per Mission Partner Brief | Named contacts preferred | High (MIS-001) | Purpose-limited |
| **Practitioner-in-Residence** | Sustained individual contribution | Repeated Mission support | Regular supervised contact | **Named** practitioner continuity | Higher onboarding | Role-based, revocable |
| **Institution / Industry-in-Residence** | Sustained **organisational** embedding across Mission cycles | Recurring Mission pipeline + critique / mentoring | Patterned, supervised | Named practitioners + institutional commitment | Highest | Role-based, time-bounded, revocable |

**Branding rule:** An organisation must **not** call itself Industry-in-Residence merely for marketing. Designation requires school recognition under this standard.

**Practitioner-in-Residence** may exist without full organisational I-in-R. **I-in-R** requires institutional commitment plus practitioner continuity.

## 4. Inheritance from MIS-001 (mandatory)

Every I-in-R is subject to applicable **APPROVED MIS-001** Mission Partner Governance in full.

Before I-in-R recognition, the organisation must satisfy MIS-001 prerequisites including:

- partner eligibility and value test;
- Education-First;
- safeguarding and learner access rules;
- IP / Creator Rights (ADR-0014);
- data boundaries;
- conflict disclosure;
- assessment / PoC non-monopoly;
- sponsorship ≠ control;
- branding / recruitment limits;
- suspension / exit acceptance;
- Mission Partner Governance literacy (implementation acknowledgement).

**MIS-002 adds requirements because the relationship is deeper, longer and more embedded** — it does not weaken MIS-001.

Explicit non-purchasable controls (reaffirm MIS-001):

| Control | I-in-R may not |
|---|---|
| Curriculum | Buy or dictate curriculum / World capture |
| Assessment | Control grades or Formal Recognition |
| PoC | Become sole verifier or demand favourable claims |
| Pathways | Assign, prohibit or steer pathways for partner needs |
| Safeguarding | Override school safeguarding |
| Stop-work | Override or bargain away school stop-work (MIS-003) |
| Learner data | Claim unrestricted profiles / analytics |
| Creator Rights / IP | Automatically own learner work |
| Branding / recruitment | Treat designation as unrestricted marketing or talent access |
| Exclusivity | Assume exclusive Mission pipeline or campus monopoly |

Where conflict arises, **APPROVED MIS-001 prevails**.

Typical path: successful **Mission Partner** engagement (or equivalent onboarding review) **before** I-in-R designation.

## 6. Embedding model and depth governance

Genuine embedding **may** include (non-mandatory set; proportional selection):

- recurring practitioner presence (physical or digital);
- ongoing Mission pipeline contribution;
- repeated technical / professional critique;
- mentoring of Mission teams under school governance;
- authentic specifications (educationalised);
- professional review sessions;
- specialist workshops **tied to live Missions**;
- supervised access to professional tools / workflows;
- design reviews and validation / testing advice;
- deployment advice within ADR-0007 Deployment Boundary;
- research collaboration under integrity rules;
- staff professional learning (Educators / Practitioners).

Because I-in-R is deeper than ordinary partnership, governance must address as applicable:

| Depth dimension | Governance posture |
|---|---|
| Duration / continuity | Planned multi-cycle engagement; review and exit rules (§16, §22) |
| Regular presence | Physical, hybrid or digital — must be real, not token (§7) |
| Repeated learner contact | Supervised, purpose-limited, briefed (§12) |
| School facilities access | Allocated/shared space governed (§13) |
| Dedicated spaces | Optional; not sufficient alone for designation |
| Tools / equipment / materials | School remains educationally responsible; return/exit rules |
| Missions / classes access | By brief only — not blanket classroom rights |
| Data access | Minimum necessary (§14) |
| Communications | School-authorised channels (§15) |
| Staff / practitioner interaction | Individual authorisation required (§10–§11) |
| Student work / IP | ADR-0014 + MIS-001 (§20) |
| Branding / recruitment | Restricted (§17, §19) |
| External-site activity | MIS-003 + Mission Brief |
| Commercial interests | Disclosed; funding ≠ control (§18) |

Not every I-in-R needs every element. Core does **not** set universal weekly hours; country/school implementation may define numeric thresholds.

## 7. Presence and recurrence

**Anti-token rule:** Visits twice a year, annual showcases, or one-off career days do **not** qualify as I-in-R.

**Core presence principle:**

> An I-in-R relationship must be sustained across **sufficient Mission cycles or developmental periods** to affect authentic learning practice — not merely event programming.

Indicators of sufficiency (principle-level, not Core hour counts):

- continuity of named practitioners;
- recurrence learners and staff can recognise;
- participation across more than a single Mission brief;
- contribution timed to Mission critique / iteration / review points;
- relationship duration measured in terms / years of planned engagement, not one calendar event.

Implementation calendars and frequency thresholds remain country/school decisions.

## 8. Practitioner continuity

Institutional logo partnership **without** working practitioners is **not** I-in-R.

| Expectation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Identifiable practitioners | Named adults responsible for contribution |
| Tarbiyat literacy | Understand Education-First, Mission architecture, and school authority |
| Safeguarding readiness | Onboarded for work with minors (§11) |
| Domain competence | Current/recent professional expertise (ADR-0015 currency principles) |
| Continuity | Same or carefully handed-over practitioners across cycles |
| Staff relationship | Known to Mission Lead / Educators / Practitioners on Mission Teams |

CEO or brand status ≠ educational authority.

## 9. Educational purpose and Mission authority

### 9.1 Required educational purpose

An I-in-R must have a defined educational purpose such as:

- authentic disciplinary exposure;
- sustained Mission contribution;
- practitioner access;
- tools / facilities / expertise;
- real-world contexts;
- feedback / critique;
- mentorship under school governance;
- capability-development opportunity.

The organisation must **not** convert learners into free labour, production staff, lead generators, sales agents, recruitment inventory, or marketing subjects (ADR-0007 / MIS-001 Education-First).

### 9.2 Permitted contributions

- supplying authentic problems (subject to educationalisation);
- helping reshape scope with Educators / Practitioners;
- technical consultation and professional standards;
- design / prototype critique;
- validation and testing advice;
- stakeholder / user insight;
- deployment support under Deployment Boundary;
- research collaboration where lawful and age-appropriate.

### 9.3 School retains authority over

- curriculum and Mission educational objectives;
- whether commercial/community problems become educational Missions;
- Mission approval and rejection;
- learner participation and voluntariness;
- scope, authenticity level, workload and deadlines;
- Formal Recognition coexistence (ADR-0003);
- assessment interpretation;
- pathway advice / non-assignment (FND-003; ADR-0015);
- safeguarding and stop-work;
- Proof of Capability decisions (ADR-0016);
- Creator Rights Review triggers (ADR-0014);
- timetable integration.

**Organisation cannot:** impose production quotas; force deadlines incompatible with learning; define success solely by commercial output; or prevent the school from educationalising, modifying or rejecting proposed work.

Authentic constraints are allowed only when educationally appropriate.

I-in-R feedback informs authenticity; it does **not** certify capability or displace Tarbiyat judgment.

## 10. Campus Live Workflow relationship

I-in-R may help create **real workflow** conditions (ADR-0007):

real specifications + practitioner standards + real review + quality expectations + suitable stakeholders.

**Real workflow ≠ free labour.**

| Required | Prohibited |
|---|---|
| Educational value first | Production quotas for learners |
| Supervised, developmentally appropriate roles | Replacing paid employees with learners |
| Inspectable learning outcomes | Repetitive low-learning commercial tasks |
| No academic penalty if commercial delivery fails | Partner-critical production dependence on children |
| Campus Live Workflow guardrails (ADR-0007) | Profit overriding learning |

## 11. People / Practitioner boundary

**An Institution is not itself a Practitioner.**

### 11.1 Separation of approvals

| Approval | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Institutional I-in-R designation** | Organisation recognised under this standard |
| **Individual adult authorisation** | Each person who works with learners must pass applicable People / safeguarding / practitioner rules |

An approved I-in-R does **NOT** automatically authorise all its employees/staff to work directly with learners.

Authority depends on **competence, onboarding, safeguarding, and defined function** — not company seniority.

### 11.2 Illustrative individual progression

`External Expert` → `Mission Partner Professional` → `Practitioner-in-Residence` → `Authorised Faculty Practitioner`

### 11.3 Hard boundaries

- I-in-R staff do not monopolise assessment;
- do not assign or prohibit pathways;
- do not override Technical / Lab Professional safety authority;
- do not act as designated safeguarding leads unless separately authorised;
- Mentor function remains school-governed longitudinal care.

Detailed Practitioner authorisation levels: `PEO-001` (`APPROVED` — **GAP-033 Closed**). Record: `MIS-FRM-007` (`APPROVED`). This approval of MIS-002 does **not** approve PEO-002–005.

## 13. Access, data and communications

### 13.1 Access model

I-in-R status does **not** automatically grant:

- unrestricted campus access;
- unsupervised learner access;
- direct personal messaging with learners;
- student personal data, PoC Profiles, pathway records, or family data;
- permanent system accounts;
- broad facility access beyond agreed spaces.

Access remains **purpose-based + role-based + time-bounded + revocable** (MIS-001).

### 13.2 Data / privacy

I-in-R status must **NOT** automatically provide:

- full learner profiles;
- academic history;
- PoC profile as analytics feed;
- contact details;
- family details;
- health / safeguarding records;
- private communications;
- unrestricted analytics.

Use purpose limitation and data minimisation. Country privacy legality: Country Profile / counsel.

### 13.3 Communications

School-authorised channels preferred. Private messaging apps / social DMs with learners are **not** default and are generally prohibited. After-hours unmanaged contact is not permitted. Photography / video / media require appropriate consent governance — designation is not promotional consent (ADR-0014 / MIS-001).

## 14. Facilities and campus presence

Illustrative models (none mandated by Core):

| Model | Notes |
|---|---|
| **A. Dedicated partner room** | Optional; not sufficient alone for I-in-R |
| **B. Shared Mission Lab presence** | Common embedding pattern |
| **C. Visiting practitioner desk** | Time-bounded desk rights |
| **D. Co-working zone** | Shared professional space under school rules |
| **E. Digital / hybrid residency** | Valid if continuity and Mission embedding are real |
| **F. External partner facility** | Learner visits only under safeguarding / Mission Brief; school remains responsible (ADR-0020 / MIS-003) |

**Physical co-location alone does not qualify** an organisation as I-in-R.

### 14.1 Facility governance checklist (as applicable)

Where space, equipment or credentials are involved, school–partner arrangements should address proportionally:

- allocated vs shared space;
- access hours;
- supervision expectations;
- equipment ownership and authorised use;
- utilities / maintenance boundaries (implementation);
- storage;
- keys / access credentials (issuance, logging, revocation);
- safety responsibility and stop-work obedience;
- visitor access through school controls;
- digital / system access;
- insurance / legal requirements (**Country**);
- removal / return of equipment on exit;
- exit restoration of school spaces.

**Architectural principle (ADR-0020):** Providing external facilities or specialist environments does **NOT** externalise the school’s educational or safeguarding responsibility. Partner premises ≠ automatic safe learning environments (MIS-003).

## 15. PoC boundary

- I-in-R may contribute observations, evidence and feedback where authorised;
- hosting a Mission does **not** make the organisation sole verifier;
- critique ≠ certification;
- employer interest ≠ PoC authority;
- PoC remains governed by APPROVED ADR-0016 (ops via `POC-001` DRAFT — not approved here).

Pressure to change PoC findings to protect partner reputation is **prohibited**.

## 16. Relationship review, accountability and quality

### 16.1 School-side accountability

Implementation must identify (names/functions are campus decisions):

| Function | Role |
|---|---|
| **Accountable owner** | School-side owner of the I-in-R relationship |
| **Approval authority** | Who may designate / renew I-in-R |
| **Review authority** | Who conducts periodic quality review |
| **Escalation** | Safeguarding / educational leadership route |
| **Suspension / termination** | Who may pause or end designation |
| **Conflict handling** | Disclosure and review path |
| **Recordkeeping** | Designation, briefs, reviews, exit records |

Avoid duplicating MIS-001 mechanisms where they already suffice; I-in-R adds designation, recurrence review and facility depth.

### 16.2 Review dimensions

Periodic review (implementation cadence; e.g. termly / annual — not Core-fixed):

- educational value;
- practitioner quality and continuity;
- learner safety;
- Mission quality;
- recurrence adequacy;
- conflicts;
- partner behaviour;
- IP / data compliance;
- learner and staff feedback;
- balance of benefit;
- whether the designation remains deserved;
- portfolio distortion risk;
- facility / access hygiene.

### 16.3 Possible outcomes

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Continue** | Maintain I-in-R |
| **Modify** | Adjust scope, Worlds, access, or practitioner set |
| **Reduce role** | Narrow Mission types or contact intensity |
| **Pause** | Temporary suspension |
| **Terminate** | End relationship |
| **Reclassify** | Downgrade to Mission Partner / Visiting Expert |

No Core composite scoring system required.

## 17. Branding and public status

I-in-R designation may be used publicly **only** when the school has recognised the relationship under this standard.

**Prevent:**

- buying the designation with sponsorship;
- sponsorship automatically conferring I-in-R;
- exaggerated partnership claims;
- implying school endorsement of company products or services;
- unrestricted logo placement as a purchaseable campus right;
- using learners, school identity or educational activity for marketing without agreement, consent, privacy and Creator Rights treatment.

Public branding must reflect the **actual governed relationship**.

---

## 18. Funding and sponsorship

I-in-R may fund equipment, Mission materials, practitioner time, labs, research, or facilities — consistent with MIS-001.

Money must **not** purchase:

- I-in-R designation;
- learner access;
- curriculum influence;
- favourable assessment or PoC results;
- automatic IP;
- exclusive recruitment rights;
- exclusive Mission pipeline;
- protection from stop-work, restriction, suspension or termination.

Procurement and educational governance remain distinguishable. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed.

---

## 19. Recruitment and employment

I-in-R is **not** primarily a recruitment programme.

| Permissible | Not permissible as hidden / default function |
|---|---|
| Career exposure | Identifying minors for recruitment |
| Professional understanding | Exclusive talent pipelines |
| Later lawful opportunities under separate school/country policy | Capability-profile / PoC harvesting |
| | Direct solicitation during compulsory learning time |
| | Conditioning Mission participation on future employment |
| | Using student data as a recruitment database |

Educational participation ≠ recruitment consent. Internships/jobs require **separate** governance. Minors/employment legality: **Country**.

---

## 20. IP, research and confidentiality

ADR-0014 + MIS-001 apply in full.

I-in-R does **not** gain automatic ownership of learner work, inventions, designs, media, code, research or portfolios — nor school IP or PoC evidence.

Funding alone must not transfer Creator Rights. Broad pre-entry IP assignment as a condition of lab access is **prohibited** as a default (case-by-case Creator Rights Review only where lawful and educationally justified).

Research collaboration may need additional review. High-confidentiality projects may be **unsuitable** for learners even if technically authentic (data classes; MIS-003).

---

## 21. Mission pipeline, Six Worlds and plural ecosystem

I-in-R may contribute a **recurring** Mission pipeline without capturing curriculum.

School maintains Mission source diversity (ADR-0007), Six Worlds coverage, partner plurality, learner choice / proportionality, Exploration Floor awareness (ADR-0006), and educational relevance over partner commercial priority.

**No single partner becomes “the curriculum.”** No Core fixed partner count or portfolio percentages.

“Industry-in-Residence” must **not** mean only technology companies. Conceptual eligibility spans all Six Worlds (university, research, public, creative, community, industry). Public/advocacy bodies remain bound by MIS-001 political neutrality.

Support multiple Institutions-in-Residence across sectors. Prevent single-company dependency, World capture, donor capture, or exclusive sector pipelines.

## 22. Continuity, exit and downgrade

### 22.1 Continuity triggers

Because I-in-R is deeper than ordinary partnership, plan for:

- unexpected withdrawal;
- insolvency;
- leadership change;
- loss of facility or specialist;
- reputational / safeguarding event;
- contractual dispute;
- school or partner termination;
- learner work already underway;
- data return / deletion;
- equipment return;
- portfolio / PoC continuity;
- replacement authentic learning arrangements;
- communications to affected learners.

**Learner continuity takes priority over institutional convenience.** Learners must not be academically harmed by partner exit (MIS-001).

### 22.2 School may downgrade, restrict, suspend or terminate when

- recurrence disappears;
- practitioner participation collapses;
- safeguarding fails;
- exploitation or commercial capture appears;
- partner becomes primarily recruitment-focused;
- conflicts become unacceptable;
- data / IP boundaries are breached;
- Mission quality deteriorates;
- branding claims exceed actual engagement;
- organisation refuses legitimate stop-work;
- persistent scope or facility violations.

**Illustrative downgrade path:**

`I-in-R` → `Mission Partner` → `Visiting Expert` → relationship ended.

Financial contribution must not shield exit.

## 23. Safeguarding / MIS-003 handoff

I-in-R does **not** bypass APPROVED MIS-003 Mission-level risk classification.

Every Mission / work package still requires appropriate risk and safeguarding review under:

`docs/06-missions-and-production/MISSION_RISK_AND_SAFEGUARDING.md` (`MIS-003`, **APPROVED**).

School retains stop-work authority for external adults, learner contact, workshop/lab/site risks, machinery/tools, travel, off-campus work, sensitive projects, hazardous materials, data exposure, photography/media, private messaging and emergencies.

**Refusal by an I-in-R to obey a legitimate stop-work decision is grounds for restriction, suspension or termination.**

Long-term partner trust ≠ blanket pre-approval. Repeated good performance may streamline implementation checklists; it must not eliminate Core controls. Do not duplicate the full MIS-003 risk matrix here.

---

## 24. Proportionality

Avoid forcing a small local organisation and a multinational into identical bureaucracy.

Governance depth should scale with:

- duration;
- learner contact;
- risk;
- data access;
- facility access;
- commercial interest;
- influence;
- volume of activity;
- safeguarding exposure.

No Core numerical score. A well-governed small local engineering firm with excellent expertise and little money may qualify; a prestigious brand offering only two publicity events per year must **not**.

---

## 25. Anti-theatre test

I-in-R must not become:

- a prestigious logo wall;
- rented branded room;
- ceremonial partnership;
- sponsor lounge;
- press-release relationship;
- nominal lab without meaningful learner access;
- company showroom disguised as education.

Require evidence of meaningful educational integration (§5–§8) before designation and at each review.
