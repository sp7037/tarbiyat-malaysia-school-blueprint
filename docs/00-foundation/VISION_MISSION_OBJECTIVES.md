## Vision (DRAFT — awaiting ratification)

Students learn by investigating, experimenting and producing through real missions in professional-grade learning environments, while remaining compatible with recognised academic credentials.

> Placeholder only. Final vision wording must be human-approved and free of unverified uniqueness claims.

## Mission (DRAFT — awaiting ratification)

To operate a Lab-First, Mission-Based educational model in which curriculum theory serves mission accomplishment; capability is evidenced longitudinally; specialisation follows broad exploration; and character, ethics, physical development and national identity are integral — without replacing the national/board recognition safety layer.

## Objectives (DRAFT list)

1. Establish Lab-First and Mission-Based learning as the primary organising logic of the student day.
2. Maintain national/board examination compatibility as formal recognition.
3. Implement Explore → Discover → Choose → Deepen → Produce progression.
4. Provide exposure across six professional/learning worlds before Major + Minor pathways.
5. Operate Competency Graph, Proof of Capability Profile and student portfolio systems.
6. Enable supervised real missions with government/community/industry partners where lawful and appropriate.
7. Support Industry-in-Residence, student IP pathways and supervised Mission-to-Market possibilities under explicit policy.
8. Retain theory/direct teaching where pedagogically required; redefine classrooms primarily as Knowledge Studios / Seminar / Briefing spaces.
