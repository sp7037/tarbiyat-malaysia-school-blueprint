## Principles (DRAFT)

1. **Production over demonstration** — practical work should produce artefacts, data, systems or services of real learning value.
2. **Mission authenticity** — prefer real problems with real stakeholders over simulated busywork, within safeguarding and law.
3. **Safety net of recognition** — never design Core features that knowingly make national/board recognition impossible.
4. **Breadth before depth** — exploration precedes irreversible specialisation.
5. **Longitudinal evidence** — capability claims about students require time-series evidence, not one-off tests alone.
6. **Dual layer documentation** — Core stays portable; country profiles carry local constraints.
7. **Pedagogical honesty** — keep direct teaching where needed; do not pretend every concept is best learned only by discovery.
8. **Language restraint** — prefer precise operational language over marketing prose.
9. **Decision traceability** — material design changes go through ADRs.
10. **Source humility** — historical Oman/India drafts inform thinking; they do not bind Malaysia by default.
