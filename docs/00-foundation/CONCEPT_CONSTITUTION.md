## Status

Skeleton of non-negotiable Core rules derived from the current project brief. Amendments require ADR + human approval.

## Article 1 — Dual architecture

The Tarbiyat Core Educational Model is universal project IP. Country implementation profiles adapt the Core; they do not redefine it silently.

## Article 2 — Lab-First and Mission-Based learning

Students learn substantially through practical production, investigation, experimentation and real missions. Laboratories, studios and workshops are primary learning environments. Classrooms function primarily as Knowledge Studios / Seminar / Briefing spaces.

## Article 3 — Theory as tool

Curriculum theory and direct teaching are retained where pedagogically required, used principally as tools for accomplishing missions rather than as the centre of school life.

## Article 4 — Recognition safety layer

A recognised national/board curriculum and examination system remains the formal recognition and academic safety layer. Core design must remain compatible with that requirement. Country-specific board choices live in country profiles.

## Article 5 — Progression before permanent labels

Student development follows **Explore → Discover → Choose → Deepen → Produce**. Broad exposure precedes specialisation. Young children must not receive premature permanent career labels. Interest and capability are discovered through longitudinal evidence.

## Article 6 — Pathways

At suitable ages (TBD), students pursue **Major + Minor** pathways within six broad professional/learning worlds:

1. Engineering & Intelligent Systems
2. Health & Life Sciences
3. Enterprise & Economics
4. Earth, Energy & Built Environment
5. Creative, Media & Human Communication
6. Society, Leadership & Public Systems

## Article 7 — Evidence of capability

The model includes a Competency Graph, a Proof of Capability Profile and a student portfolio, complementary to — not a silent replacement for — formal academic recognition.

## Article 8 — Missions, industry and enterprise

Real government/community/industry missions, Industry-in-Residence, student intellectual property pathways, and Mission-to-Market / supervised micro-venture possibilities are Core-enabled features. Legal mechanics are country-profile matters.

## Article 9 — Character and identity

Character, ethics, physical development and national identity are integral. Specific national identity content is defined in country profiles.

## Article 10 — Claims discipline

Uniqueness, approval, partnership and performance claims require evidence IDs. Unverified claims must not enter APPROVED Core text.
