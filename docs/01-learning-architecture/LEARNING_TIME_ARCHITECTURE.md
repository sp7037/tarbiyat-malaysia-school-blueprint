## Purpose

Explain how Tarbiyat organises learner time so the school can be Lab-First and Mission-Based while retaining Formal Recognition and disciplinary depth **without double workload**.

This is **architecture**, not a campus timetable and not a Malaysia schedule.

---

## Core idea in one paragraph

Tarbiyat runs a **single integrated programme** composed of **Learning-Time Modes** (Mission, Knowledge Studio, Recognition Preparation, Mentoring/Pathway Dialogue, Reflection/Documentation/Evidence, Formation, Independent/Collaborative Practice). Modes may be dedicated, rotational or embedded. Mission Mode is the primary organising logic of the Learning Engine over a meaningful window; Knowledge Studios remain essential for theory, deliberate practice and examination preparation; Formal Recognition may intensify in bounded **Recognition Seasons** without permanently abolishing Missions. Six Worlds are not six extra subjects. Intensity rises across Explore→Produce without Core fixed percentages; declared intensity must be staffable.
