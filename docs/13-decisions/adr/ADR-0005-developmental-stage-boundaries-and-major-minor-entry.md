## Title

Developmental Stage Boundaries and Major-Minor Entry Architecture

## Status

`UNDER REVIEW` — not APPROVED.

## Context

ADR-0004 (UNDER REVIEW) defines the five stages **Explore → Discover → Choose → Deepen → Produce** conceptually, establishes the Exploration Floor, progressive reversibility, Six Worlds discovery exposure, and Major + Minor as the preferred later-stage pathway architecture **in principle**. It deliberately left open:

- typical developmental windows for each stage (`GAP-003`);
- when formal Major + Minor becomes appropriate (`GAP-004`).

Without those Core boundaries:

- country profiles may invent early irreversible tracking;
- a strong early interest may be misread as a permanent occupational label;
- meaningful depth may be delayed so late that advanced capability cannot form before exit;
- Formal Recognition Layer obligations (ADR-0003) may be designed as if they replace pathway architecture, or vice versa.

This ADR answers the Core developmental question:

> At approximately what developmental periods should Explore, Discover, Choose, Deepen and Produce dominate, and when should formal Major + Minor pathway selection become appropriate?

It does **not** map stages to Malaysian Years, invent Malaysian regulations, set timetable percentages, define Competency Graph schemas, or draft Six Worlds competency maps.

**Dependency note:** This ADR assumes ADR-0004’s stage meanings and constraints. If ADR-0004 is materially revised before approval, this ADR must be re-checked.

## Decision

Tarbiyat Core adopts the following developmental-boundary and Major-Minor entry architecture:

1. Stages use **typical developmental windows** with **overlapping transition ranges**, not birthday cut-offs and not mandatory individual placement by exact age alone.
2. Progression is governed by **both** typical developmental window **and** readiness evidence under human governance.
3. Formal **Major + Minor** begins at entry to **Deepen**, typically in middle-to-later adolescence — **not** during Explore, Discover, or Choose.
4. **Choose** permits **provisional concentration** (increased depth without named Major/Minor commitment).
5. Commitment ladder is:  
   **Interest → Preference → Provisional Concentration → Major/Minor → Advanced Production**
6. Stages may overlap functionally: a learner has a **dominant stage** while secondary functions from adjacent stages continue.
7. Country profiles later perform:  
   **Core Development Stage → Country Age/Year Structure → Recognition Requirements**  
   Core stage architecture is not a country Year map.

### Boundary design choice (explicit)

| Approach | Adopted? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Exact age cut-offs as mandatory placement rules | No | Misallocates late bloomers; conflicts with readiness and reversibility |
| Completely age-free progression | No | Removes planning anchors for curriculum intensity, safeguarding and recognition coexistence |
| Approximate age/developmental windows + readiness + overlapping transitions | **Yes** | Defensible Core planning without false precision |

**Typical window ≠ mandatory individual placement rule.** Windows guide programme design. Individual placement uses readiness review, safeguarding and human dialogue.

## Developmental architecture table

Ages are **approximate typical windows**, not birthday gates. Transition may begin earlier or later according to readiness, country structure and safeguarding. Claims about precise normative ages remain subject to evidence verification (`GAP-021`).

| Stage | Typical developmental window | Dominant purpose | Specialisation level | Pathway status | Typical mission authenticity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Explore** | Approx. early childhood through later primary years (**~5–11**), with soft transition into Discover often beginning ~10–12 | Broad foundations: language, mathematics, making, inquiry, culture, physical development, creativity, collaboration, ethical/civic formation | Breadth-dominant; depth only as emerging curiosity | **No** formal pathway; interests recorded formatively only | Teacher-designed / authentic-context activities; short missions |
| **Discover** | Approx. later primary / early adolescence (**~10–13**), overlapping Choose | Structured exposure across **all Six Worlds** via rotations and comparative missions; guided reflection | Still breadth-first; comparative depth samples only | Preferences may emerge; **no** Major/Minor; **no** permanent World label | Realistic simulations and observed community problems |
| **Choose** | Approx. early-to-middle adolescence (**~12–15**), overlapping Discover and Deepen | Preferences become visible; students may begin **provisional concentrations** | Moderate concentration permitted; Exploration Floor mandatory | **Provisional concentration only**; highly reversible; not occupational commitment | Limited safeguarded external stakeholder work may begin where appropriate |
| **Deepen** | Approx. middle-to-later adolescence (**~14–17**), overlapping Choose and Produce | Meaningful specialisation; technical/theoretical depth; interdisciplinary missions | High but bounded specialisation | **Formal Major + Minor may begin** after readiness review | Increasingly authentic external problems under law and safeguarding |
| **Produce** | Approx. later adolescence (**~16–18+**), overlapping Deepen | Advanced capability demonstration: capstones, research, prototypes, enterprise/policy/media outputs, increasingly authentic external work | Advanced production within Major/Minor + Exploration Floor | Major/Minor typically active; still reversible with bridging; **not** a permanent career assignment by the school | Genuine industry / government / university / community missions where safe, appropriate and lawful |

**Notes on the table**

- Overlaps are intentional (e.g. Discover↔Choose ~12–13; Choose↔Deepen ~14–15; Deepen↔Produce ~16–17).
- Formal Recognition Layer subjects and assessments continue across stages where required (ADR-0003).
- Mission authenticity remains a principle only; stage gates stay open (`GAP-018`).

## Stage transition principles

1. **Dominant stage** — At any time, one stage dominates programme design and expectations.
2. **Secondary continuing functions** — Adjacent-stage activities continue (e.g. Discover rotations may continue into early Choose; Deepen retains Exploration Floor; Produce retains academic and human-development requirements).
3. **Overlapping transition ranges** — Movement is a window, not a single day event.
4. **Readiness review** — Transition into Choose provisional concentration, and especially into Deepen Major/Minor, requires human-governed readiness evidence (see below).
5. **Reversibility** — Earlier preferences and provisional concentrations must not permanently trap the learner (`GAP-020` for bridging detail).
6. **No permanent label** — Stage placement and World concentration are educational states, not occupational identities.
7. **Safeguarding and recognition override convenience** — Transition must not bypass safety, wellbeing or Formal Recognition Layer obligations.

Stages are overlapping developmental emphases, not five isolated boxes.

---

## Readiness principle

Progression uses **both**:

### A. Typical age / developmental window

Programme design, staffing, mission intensity and pathway ceremonies are planned against the windows above.

### B. Readiness evidence (architecture-level indicators)

Indicators may include, without becoming a Competency Graph schema:

- foundational learning sufficiency for the next stage’s demands;
- demonstrated engagement over time (not a single event);
- emerging self-reflection appropriate to age;
- sustained interest where concentration is proposed;
- prerequisite competencies for proposed depth areas;
- capacity for increasing independence under supervision;
- mentor observational evidence;
- maturity and safeguarding considerations;
- student participation in dialogue; family dialogue where age-appropriate.

**Rules:**

1. Readiness evidence informs human judgment; it does not auto-promote.
2. No single test, score, AI output, parent preference or teacher preference independently determines stage or pathway (FND-003; ADR-0004).
3. A learner outside the typical window may transition earlier or later if readiness and safeguarding support it.
4. Detailed scoring models and database schemas are out of scope (`GAP-009`).

## Major + Minor entry rule

### Resolved (proposed Core rule for `GAP-004`)

| Commitment level | When appropriate | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Interest | Explore onward | Emerging curiosity; formative only |
| Preference | Discover → Choose | Evidenced leaning; not commitment |
| **Provisional concentration** | **Choose** (typically ~12–15) | Increased time/depth in selected areas; Exploration Floor continues; **no** named Major/Minor |
| **Formal Major + Minor** | **Deepen onward** (typically from ~14–17 entry) | Named Major depth + complementary Minor; still reversible with bridging |
| Advanced production | Produce (overlapping Deepen) | Capstones and authentic outputs within Major/Minor + recognition obligations |

**Entry conditions for formal Major + Minor (architecture):**

1. Learner is in (or entering) the Deepen dominant stage.
2. Discover-phase Six Worlds exposure has been meaningfully undertaken (or equivalent bridging if a late entrant).
3. Readiness review completed under human governance with student participation.
4. Exploration Floor remains in force.
5. Formal Recognition Layer pathway remains feasible in the country profile (ADR-0003).
6. No AI/algorithm/test/adult independently assigns the Major or Minor.

**Deliberately rejected for formal Major/Minor start:** Explore, Discover, and Choose as the formal naming stage.

---

## Reversibility / bridging relationship

1. Provisional concentrations in Choose are expected to change for many learners.
2. Major/Minor in Deepen/Produce is progressively more consequential but remains **reasonably reversible** with prerequisite bridging (`GAP-020`).
3. A change (e.g. Engineering concentration → Enterprise Major) is a pathway transition, not a failure and not a permanent career reversal narrative.
4. Bridging may include academic catch-up required by the Formal Recognition Layer.
5. Reversibility does not mean frictionless instant switching without learning consequences; it means the architecture must provide a designed bridge rather than lock-in.

---

## Exploration Floor relationship

- Exploration Floor (ADR-0004) applies at **all** stages, including Deepen and Produce.
- Formal Major + Minor **increases** specialisation; it does **not** authorise zero breadth.
- Operational minimums / non-percentage packages remain open (`GAP-019`; related `GAP-016`).

---

## Relationship to Six Worlds

1. **Discover** requires meaningful structured exposure across all Six Worlds.
2. **Choose** provisional concentration may lean toward one or more Worlds without exclusive lock-in.
3. **Deepen / Produce** Major + Minor typically sit within one or more Worlds and may be interdisciplinary.
4. Worlds are domains for exploration and concentration — **not** permanent career labels for children.
5. Detailed World competency maps drafted in ADR-0011 (`UNDER REVIEW`; `GAP-008` proposed resolution — not closed).

---

## Relationship to Formal Recognition Layer

1. Stage and Major/Minor architecture organise the Tarbiyat Learning Engine; they do not abolish applicable statutory or recognised requirements (ADR-0003).
2. A country may require compulsory subjects through the end of secondary schooling; Major/Minor must **coexist** with those requirements, not replace them.
3. Country mapping pattern:  
   `Core stage` → `local age/Year structure` → `recognition subjects/assessments/credentials`
4. This ADR does not invent Malaysian Years, boards or exam pathways (`GAP-002` remains open).

---

## Human oversight / safeguarding

1. Mentors, educators and governors remain accountable for stage transitions, pathway advice, wellbeing and evidence integrity.
2. Family dialogue informs but does not alone determine pathway.
3. **No aptitude test, AI system, algorithm, parent, teacher or mentor may independently determine a student's pathway** (FND-003; ADR-0004).
4. Systems may recommend; humans decide with student participation appropriate to age.
5. Safeguarding intensity rises with mission authenticity and external work (see `GAP-018`).
6. Premature commercialisation or unsafe deployment is not required for Produce-stage validity (ADR-0002 / ADR-0004).

## Rationale

### Developmental reasoning (material findings only)

1. **Early childhood / primary:** Breadth, foundational literacy/numeracy, play/making/inquiry and identity formation dominate. Premature vocational tracking risks false permanent labels and weakens later mobility — aligning with FND-003 prohibition on permanent career labelling of young children.
2. **Early adolescence:** Interests emerge but remain relatively fluid; structured multi-domain exposure (Discover) is more appropriate than named pathway commitment.
3. **Comparative education on tracking:** Across OECD systems, first placement into differentiated upper-secondary-style pathways commonly clusters around mid-adolescence (~15–16), while very early selection (~10–12) is associated with equity and late-bloomer misallocation risks. Tarbiyat therefore delays **formal** Major/Minor until Deepen, while still allowing provisional concentration earlier. *(Comparative pattern informs Core timing; it is not a country statute. Verification pack: `GAP-021`.)*
4. **Middle adolescence:** Stronger preferences, increasing reflective capacity and readiness for technical depth support Choose → Deepen transition — provisional then formal commitment.
5. **Later adolescence:** Sustained specialisation, advanced projects and authentic external work fit Produce, without treating school pathway as a final occupational assignment.
6. **Balance:** Starting formal Major/Minor only in the final school years would under-serve advanced capability formation; starting it in Choose or earlier would recreate premature tracking under a new name.

### Why provisional concentration in Choose + formal Major/Minor in Deepen

- Protects 10–12-year-olds from permanent occupational framing.
- Allows 13–15-year-olds meaningful depth without false permanence.
- Gives Deepen/Produce enough runway for advanced capability before exit.
- Preserves Major + Minor as preferred architecture (ADR-0004) without reopening whether it exists.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Closes the Core timing ambiguity for GAP-003/004 at architectural level (subject to human approval of this ADR).
- Gives country profiles a portable mapping target without Malaysian Year invention.
- Aligns specialisation timing with Exploration Floor, reversibility and dual recognition.
- Provides implementable stage intensity guidance for later timetable work (`GAP-016`) without fixing percentages here.

### Risks / costs

- Approximate windows may be misread as birthday laws if country profiles are careless.
- Readiness reviews need operational design later or they become rubber stamps.
- Dependency on ADR-0004 remaining coherent through its own approval.
- Comparative-education grounding needs a cited evidence pack (`GAP-021`) before over-claiming universality of the age numbers.

### Follow-on work

- Human review/approval of this ADR and of ADR-0004.
- Evidence verification pack for developmental windows (`GAP-021`).
- Malaysia mapping only after Core ratification: stage → MY Year → recognition (`GAP-002`, country profile).
- Exploration Floor operationalisation (`GAP-019`); bridging standards (`GAP-020`); authenticity gates (`GAP-018`); Six Worlds maps (`GAP-008`).

## Alternatives Considered

1. **Exact age cut-offs as mandatory placement** — Rejected: false precision; harms late bloomers; conflicts with readiness principle.
2. **Completely age-free progression** — Rejected: insufficient for Core programme design, safeguarding planning and recognition coexistence.
3. **Early formal specialisation (Major/Minor in primary / early Discover)** — Rejected: premature labelling; equity/tracking risks; conflicts with FND-003 and ADR-0004.
4. **Delay all formal specialisation until final school years only** — Rejected: insufficient runway for advanced Deepen/Produce capability.
5. **Major + Minor beginning in Choose** — Rejected as formal start: Choose should remain highly reversible provisional concentration; naming Major/Minor too early recreates lock-in pressure.
6. **Provisional concentration in Choose with formal Major + Minor in Deepen** — **Accepted** as preferred Core rule.
7. **Single-track Major-only from Deepen** — Not reopened here; Major + Minor remains preferred in principle per ADR-0004.
